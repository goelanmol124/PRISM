## Usage Notes

1. These tracks are used as background music in PRISM-generated videos
2. The audio ducking system automatically lowers music volume during speech
3. Music is selected randomly from available tracks in this folder
4. Supported formats: .mp3, .wav, .m4a

## License Compliance

When publishing videos created with PRISM that include this music:

1. Include the attribution text in your video description
2. For CC BY 4.0 tracks, credit is REQUIRED for free use
3. Pro licenses can be purchased from artist websites for no-attribution use

## Adding New Tracks

Place new royalty-free music files in this folder. Ensure:
- The track has a compatible license (CC BY, CC0, or purchased license)
- Update this CREDITS.txt file with attribution information
- Preferred format: MP3 320kbps or WAV
